Click a hotspot on the page to open a question tied to that exact part of the PDF page.

Add hotspots over important definitions, equations, figures, or paragraphs. This feels very natural inside Notion because the page stays clean and white while the interaction lives in a soft side panel.

Best embed look in Notion: use a transparent page background, a white paper card, soft border, and avoid dark app chrome. Host on Vercel or GitHub Pages, then paste the URL into a Notion embed block.
